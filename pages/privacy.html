KUKA HOME (kukahome.uz) is a premium furniture retail chain operating in Uzbekistan. This page explains what information we exchange with customers who contact us through Facebook, Instagram, Telegram, and other messaging channels.

2. Information we collect

When you message us via Instagram Direct, Facebook Messenger, a comment on one of our posts, or Telegram, we collect:

  • Your name and profile picture (from your social media profile, to the extent you've made it available)
  • The content of your messages, and any photos or files you send

  • Order and purchase history, if you become a registered customer

3. How we use it

  • To answer your questions and advise you on our products

Conversation and customer data is kept for the duration of the customer relationship, and afterward as required for accounting/warranty purposes (typically up to 3 years).
